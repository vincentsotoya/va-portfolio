# VA Portfolio

A personal portfolio site for a technical virtual assistant: administrative and executive-assistant help, backed by a software engineering background.

## Language

**Owner**:
Vincent Sotoya, the person the site represents and who is hired through it.

**Visitor**:
A prospective client reading the site, mainly from the US and other English-speaking countries.

**Service**:
A named kind of admin help the Owner offers (Email Management, Calendar Management, Document & Data Entry, Workflow Automation). Lists only tools the Owner has actually used, and is backed by one **Sample**.
_Avoid_: Project, product

**Sample**:
A small demonstration workflow for a Service, on its own page with fake data (before and after), always labelled as a sample. Not client work. The Clip illustrates a Service; the Sample proves it.
_Avoid_: Case study, client project

**Clip**:
A short silent motion graphic, made in Remotion, that shows what a Service does. Scroll-driven: the Visitor's scroll moves it forward.

**Availability**:
The Owner's current hiring status shown to Visitors (Available, Limited or Booked), set by hand.

**Base**:
Where the Owner works from: the Philippines.

**Intro Call**:
A 15-minute call a Visitor books through the calendar link to discuss working together.
_Avoid_: Interview, consultation

**Post**:
A blog entry about the Owner's life in Japan. Photos of the Owner only; no employer or project names.
_Avoid_: Article

**Credentials**:
The résumé-derived proof of reliability shown to Visitors (years in software engineering, trained as a Java engineer in Japan, basic Japanese only). Never names the Owner's employers' clients or projects.
_Avoid_: Testimonials (there are none yet)

## Relationships

- A **Service** has one **Sample** and one **Clip**.
- Every **Post** ends with a link to book an **Intro Call**.
- **Availability** and **Credentials** appear on the home page, not on Posts.
